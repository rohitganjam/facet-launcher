# 0.1.11

## New
- Show a widget from another app on Home in place of the clock, with the same drag-to-move and resize controls as other widgets.

## Improved
- Long-pressing an icon on Home, the dock or the drawer now opens its menu when you lift your finger, with a haptic buzz when the long-press is recognized.
- Apps inside folders now respond to long-press the same way as icons everywhere else.
