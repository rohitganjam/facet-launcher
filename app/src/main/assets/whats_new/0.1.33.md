# 0.1.33

## New
- Two new icon styles, Glyph and Outline, draw each app's logo as a filled shape or as line art. Pick the colour (Accent, Theme or Theme inverted) and, if you like, put the icon on a tile, or a reversed tile, in Settings → Appearance → Icons.
- The Icons page shows a preview of your own apps in every style, so you can compare before you choose.

## Improved
- Themes and colors, Fonts and Icons are now three rows under General
- The app menu collapses notifications more smoothly, swipe a notification away to clear it or use "Clear all", and tap the header to open the app.
- The Home clock no longer ends up smaller than it should after the screen finishes laying out.
