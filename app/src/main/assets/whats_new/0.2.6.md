    # 0.2.6

    ## New
    - Choose from three new clock styles: Echo, Day Line and Liquid Hour.
    - Long-press anywhere on the clock's box to open the clock's settings.

    ## Improved
    - The clock can now be resized to any width and height, and its text scales to fit the box. The background panel follows the box you sized the clock to.
    - With a background panel, calendar events stay aligned to the left on a centered clock.
    - The clock style gallery now opens on the style you're currently using.
    - Clock accessories now live in the clock's settings.
    - The app no longer fails when Bluetooth permission has been revoked.
