# 0.1.18

## New
- Facet automation: rules that switch your facet for you. Add them in Settings → Facet automation, to run on a schedule (days and times, overnight allowed), when you connect to a Wi-Fi network or a Bluetooth device, plug in headphones, or reach a battery level. Picking a facet yourself, or switching with a shortcut from an app like Tasker, overrides the active rule until it ends.
- Facet Pro, a one-time purchase through Google Play with no subscription: up to 10 facets, automation rules for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, headphones and battery, and your own widget in place of the clock. Restore it on any phone signed in to the same Google account.
- Icon shape: choose Squircle, Rounded, Circle or Square for app icons in Appearance.

## Improved
- Going back now slides the previous screen in on every screen.
- Home text (clock, calendar and app labels) has a softer shadow, so it stays readable on light wallpapers.
- The clock style option, with a live preview, now lives in Appearance.
- The Permissions screen lists Bluetooth and Location, which automation rules can use.
