# 0.1.17

## New
- A "System bar icons" setting (Appearance → General) controls the color of the status and navigation bar icons, so they stay readable over light wallpapers: Match theme, Light or Dark.
- Clock adjust mode now has an Alignment option (left, center or right), as well as height and scale.
- A hint shows you can press and hold the clock to resize or move it.

## Improved
- Less blank space at the bottom of the app drawer, so the list scrolls closer to the search bar.
- The clock resize handle is easier to see against any wallpaper.
- A System settings shortcut in the drawer's overflow menu.
