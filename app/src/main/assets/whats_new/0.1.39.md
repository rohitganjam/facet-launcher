# 0.1.39

## New
- **Facet widgets**: Weather, Agenda and Now playing are built in. Place them on the Hub or Home, then resize, move, replace or remove them.
- Weather and Agenda widgets can have their **own location or calendars**, and open their settings when you add them.
- **Long-press a widget** (clock, calendar or Facet widget) for its settings, size and position, replace, remove and the widget stack.
- **Long-press an empty area** of Home for your facet's settings and Launcher settings.
- Each **clock has its own settings**, and you can add it back from the widget picker.
- Clock styles show as a row of templates at the top of the style screen.
- **Search sheet**: the drawer's search bar shows recently installed or updated, frequently used and last used apps (needs usage access). Results show settings and contacts as tiles, with icons and photos.
- **Work Profile**: Agenda and the clock show work calendars, the widget picker has Personal and Work tabs, and tapping a work event opens the work Calendar app.
- **Weather settings** has its own row in Settings > System, and each facet can set its own clock weather location.
- **Copy to facets** applies the default dock, apps list and look to the facets you pick.
- Onboarding has a **notifications step** for badges and the Home media player.
- **Each facet keeps its own dock, apps and look.** New facets start from your launcher defaults, and Reset to default copies them back in.
- Settings lead with your **active facet's settings**, and Facets is now **Manage Facets**.

## Improved
- App grids in the drawer, Home and folders have full-width labels, bigger text and tighter spacing, so more fits.
- Long song titles in Now playing scroll.
- Settings are reorganized: a **Customization** card with **Launcher appearance**, and Dock & Apps appearance is now **Dock & Apps layout**.
