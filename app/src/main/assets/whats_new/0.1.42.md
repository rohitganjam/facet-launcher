# 0.1.42

## New
- The dock, apps list, calendar, media player row and widgets can now sit on a glass background, in different variants.
- A new Wallpaper screen lets you pick a photo, pan and zoom it to fit, and set it as your wallpaper, so glass backgrounds can show a real blur of it.
- Already have a wallpaper you like? Choose "Use my current wallpaper" and follow the steps to take a screenshot of it, or pick an image you already have.
- Icons can now use glass backgrounds too, in the same styles as the dock.
- The Weather, Agenda and Now playing widgets each have a Background option in their settings, and Now playing now has its own settings.
- The Dock, Apps list and Widgets screens have a new "Copy from facet" option that copies another facet's setup in one step.
- The Agenda widget now shows the whole coming week, and each day after Tomorrow gets a dated heading like "14th Nov, Sat".

## Improved
- Previews in settings now show your own wallpaper instead of the phone's original one.
- The clock no longer shows a 0% battery reading before it gets the real battery level.
- The landscape layout settings now sit together in one card with clearer names.
