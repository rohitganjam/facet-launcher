# 0.2.2

## New
- **App Vault:** hide apps behind a 4-digit PIN. Open it from the app drawer's 3-dot menu to view hidden apps, open their App info or remove them from the vault.
- **Custom app icons:** change any app's icon from its long-press menu, using the app's own icon, one from an installed icon pack, or any icon a pack offers. Reset them in Settings > Icons > Custom icons.
- **Play Store search:** when your search in the app drawer finds nothing, you can search in the Play Store with one click.
- **Wallpaper capture:** capture your wallpaper at once once so glass effects can show it, with separate copies for each screen and orientation, including foldables.
- **Frosted panels:** the app drawer, widget hub and facet carousel now appear as frosted glass over your wallpaper, when glass effects are enabled.
- **Smart search:** it handles accents, typos in word order, initials and multi-word searches, learns your picks, and uses Facet's own launch history, so Usage Access permission is no longer needed. 
- **Quick Access from search**: Press enter at any time to open the first result in the list: the first app, or the first contact or setting when no app matches.


## Improved
- **Wallpaper colours:** now available on Android 11 too.
- **Wallpapers you set:** they now match what your screen shows, and the preview follows your screen's shape, so wide or unfolded screens no longer stretch photos.
- **Daily photos:** pictures that would look too enlarged are skipped.
- **Closing the drawer with Home:** the search panel now slides out smoothly instead of flashing back.
- **Facet Pro screens:** they now list every Pro feature accurately.
- **Automation rules in backups:** your rules are now backed up with everything else, and the backup screen tells you about any that couldn't be restored.
- **Recently installed icon:** it is drawn like any app icon, so your icon style, tile, glass and shape apply to it, and its sheet shows that icon instead of a folder tile.
- **App Drawer and Search:** the App Drawer entry in Settings is renamed to match what it holds.
