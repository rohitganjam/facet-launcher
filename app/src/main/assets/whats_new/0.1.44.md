# 0.1.44

## New
- Browse today's photos from Bing and Spotlight on the wallpaper screen, and set any of them as your wallpaper.
- Let your wallpaper change by itself once a day to a fresh Bing or Spotlight photo (Facet Pro).

## Improved
- Swiping sideways on a Facet widget in the clock stack, like the Agenda, now turns to the next page.
- Glass panels no longer draw a copy of your wallpaper over the real one.
- In the two-column Home list, glass columns now have the same gap as glass rows.
