# 0.1.14

## New
- Home's app list can now be two columns or a grid (4, 5 or 6 columns), as well as the original single column. Each layout has its own alignment and icon and text options in Settings → Appearance.
- You can now have up to 12 favorites, up from 6.
- Switch to a facet straight from a deep link or a long-press home-screen shortcut.

## Improved
- A facet's settings header button now shows a checkmark and "Active" when that facet is applied, or "Activate" when it isn't.
- A cleaner look: the heading above Home's app list and the "Preview" label on settings previews are gone.
