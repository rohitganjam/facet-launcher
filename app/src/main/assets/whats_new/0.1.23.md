# 0.1.23

## New
- Choose whether calendar events on Home show a colored bar, and how many events to show (1 to 3), for the whole launcher or per facet.
- A live preview at the top of the Calendars screen shows your real upcoming events once you've granted access, or sample events when there are none.

## Improved
- Home now shows events from the start of today until noon tomorrow instead of today only, with a weekday label such as "Tue 9:00" on events that aren't today.
