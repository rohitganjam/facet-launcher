# 0.2.1

## New
- Settings > Gestures now lets you pick what each swipe on Home does: open the app drawer, open the Hub, switch facets, open the notification shade, or nothing at all.

## Improved
- Resizing a Hub widget is now smooth: it follows your finger, outlines the cells it will take, and glides into place when you let go.
- The facet carousel now updates right away when you change your wallpaper, instead of showing the old one until you reopen Home.
