# 0.1.43

## New
- The dock, apps list, clock and its events, media player row, widgets and icons can now sit on a glass background, in different styles, with a real blur of your wallpaper behind them.
- A new Wallpaper screen lets you pick a photo, pan and zoom it to fit, and set it as your wallpaper, or let Facet use your current wallpaper by taking a screenshot of it, so glass backgrounds can blur it.
- The clock has a single Background setting in Clock Style that covers both the time and your events.
- The Weather, Agenda and Now playing widgets each have a Background option, including a solid Theme or Theme inverted look, and a Text option to match your theme or always use light or dark text. Now playing now has its own settings.
- The Dock, Apps list and Widgets screens have a new "Copy from facet" option that copies another facet's setup in one step.

## Improved
- The Agenda widget now shows the whole coming week, and each day after Tomorrow gets a dated heading like "14th Nov, Sat".
- Previews in settings now show your own wallpaper instead of the phone's original one.
- In the single-column app list, rows now line up at a consistent width.
- The clock no longer shows a 0% battery reading before it gets the real battery level.
- The landscape layout settings now sit together in one card with clearer names.
