# 0.1.20

## New
- Manage Facets now opens with a Facet automation card showing how many rules you have, which takes you straight to your automation rules.

## Improved
- The Facet Pro screen ends with Add a facet and Restore purchases buttons that scroll with the page. Restore purchases re-checks Google Play at any time and tells you what it found.
- Pro now comes only from Google Play. Your Pro status is no longer saved in Android's automatic backups or phone-to-phone transfers: after restoring to a new phone, Facet checks Google Play on first launch, and your settings, facets and rules come back as before.
