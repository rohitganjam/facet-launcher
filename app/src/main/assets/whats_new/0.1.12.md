# 0.1.12

## New
- The dock and app list style and position can now be set for each facet, with an Inherit / Override switch in each facet's settings.
- Three new fonts, Montserrat, Lato and Inter, for the clock and calendar and for the whole launcher.

## Improved
- Appearance settings are redesigned into three cards (Dock & Home, Clock and General), with a live preview of your favorites, dock and calendar.
- The Roboto Flex Narrow clock is polished: a lighter weight, and the alarm and battery readouts stacked vertically.
