# 0.1.24

## Improved
- The media player row now disappears when you dismiss its notification, even if the app keeps playing in the background.
- With several players available, the row shows the one that's playing, or the one you played last.
- Home now fades away behind the Widgets and Switch Facets screens on every Android version, the same way it does behind the app drawer.
- A little more space between the calendar and the media player row.
