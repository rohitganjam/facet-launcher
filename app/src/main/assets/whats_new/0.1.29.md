# 0.1.29

## New
- Landscape mode: Home now has a dedicated landscape layout, with its own clock, app list and dock, so the launcher looks right when you turn your device.
- Tablet and foldable support: Home, the facet carousel and the previews adapt to wide and large screens. Launcher settings stays visible in the carousel, and the previews show your actual Home screen.

## Improved
- Home appears faster when the launcher starts.
- App Drawer settings have moved under Appearance.
- Custom widgets on Home can now be resized down to the size of one Hub grid cell.
- Backups now include your clock and app list settings, including the ones for landscape.
- Saving a backup over an older one no longer corrupts the file, and backups damaged this way can still be restored.
