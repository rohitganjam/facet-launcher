# 0.2.5

## New
- The Now playing widget now adapts to its size, with six layouts from a small tile up to a full player.

## Improved
- Daily photos now come straight from Microsoft's Bing and Spotlight feeds, matched to your phone's language and region.
- The source and profile switches are easier to see.
- On a landscape Hub, widgets no longer shift left when you pull the Hub in.
- On a landscape Home screen, the apps list now has a proper right margin when there's no dock beside it.
- Resizing a widget on the landscape Hub no longer snaps back when you drag it too wide.
