# 0.1.30

## New
- Weather can now appear on your clock next to the alarm and battery, showing the current icon and temperature.
- Tap the weather on your clock to see a 24-hour forecast, more details and the outlook for the days ahead.
- Use your current location or search for a city, and save up to 10 cities to switch between.
- Weather icons come in two styles: the standard monochrome one, or colour.
- New Clock accessories settings turn the alarm, battery and weather on or off, with a live preview of your clock.
- App drawer settings now show a live preview of your drawer, and you can drag it to scroll through the sample apps.

## Improved
- The previews in Appearance settings now show the alarm, battery and weather row.
- The "Airy" app drawer grid density is now called "Spacious", and your current choice stays the same.
- Folders in the app drawer grid now line up in the centre like app icons.
- Small date and accessory text on the clock is now a bit bigger, so it's easier to read.
- If you haven't moved the clock divider yourself, it now sits a bit lower on the home screen by default.
