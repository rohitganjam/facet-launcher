# 0.1.41

## New
- Edit Home in place: press and hold a Home row, Dock icon or open-folder row to drag it into a new order, and release in place to open its options.
- Drag items around Home's grid: drop one on an empty spot to move it, or on another item to swap, with the grid scrolling at the edges as you drag.
- Switch facet and Adjust widget size & position are now in the Home sheet.
- The drawer preview in setup and App drawer settings now shows your own installed apps with their real icons.
- Swipe between tabs in the Drawer and the app and widget pickers, with the neighbouring tab sliding into view as you go.
- Folder settings now have a menu to rename a folder, add it to or remove it from the Dock or Favorites, or delete it.

## Improved
- Each facet now has its own Dock and Favorites, and a new facet starts with your device's default apps in its Dock.
- Setup has a separate "Set up your app drawer" step with a preview and a Folders in drawer option, and the home step is now called "Set up your first facet".
- Dock style now lives on the Dock screen and the apps list look on the Apps list screen, replacing the separate Dock & Apps layout page.
- The Home grid now spans the screen like the Dock, with rows matching the App Drawer's row height and evenly spaced Dock icons.
- Home fades smoothly as you pull down the notification shade.
- Allow landscape mode is now set per facet, on its Apps list screen, so one facet can rotate while another stays in portrait.
- Weather can now name your neighbourhood along with your city.
- A full Dock or Favorites now shows a "limit reached" row, and new items take the first free spot.
- Favorites and Apps can now show up to 20 items.
- The App drawer's default grid density is now Regular.
- Home swipes no longer interfere while you're holding an item.
- Your Home grid layout is now included in backups and restored on import.
