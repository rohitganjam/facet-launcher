# 0.1.22

## New
- An optional media player row on your home screen, with album art, song details, playback controls and a progress bar (flat or wavy). Turn it on in Appearance, under Notifications and media.

## Improved
- Appearance settings are now a clearer hub: Clock & calendar, Dock & home, Notifications and media, and General.
- Settings rows now tell you when notification or calendar access still needs to be granted.
- Refined clock designs: the Accent Field clock lines up its hour and minute, the Ruler clock puts the date and extra info on their own line, and date and accessory text are easier to read.
- The new and updated settings are translated into German, Spanish, French and Portuguese.
