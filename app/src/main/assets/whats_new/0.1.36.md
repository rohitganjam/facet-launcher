# 0.1.36

## New
- You can now hide the status bar on Home, Hub and the app drawer; swipe down on Home to show it for a few seconds, and swipe again to open your notifications.
- With an icon pack applied, the new Force icon shape switch gives the pack's icons your chosen shape.

## Improved
- Icon shape and Icon size now sit together above the icon pack options and work whether or not you use an icon pack.
- Pressing Home while you're in another app no longer opens drawer search by mistake.
- In Appearance, the "General" section is now called "Look & Feel".
