# Facet Launcher 0.1.16

## New Features
- (none)

## Fixes
- Fixed an issue where touching and holding an app or folder to open its menu could also accidentally trigger the Hub or app carousel to open behind it.

## Improvements
- Single-column rows of apps and folders are now narrower (80% width) so there's empty space next to them, and that empty space can be touched and held to bring up the same adjustment menu as long-pressing the clock.
- Adjusted how swipes are detected on the Home screen for better accuracy, and swiping sideways starting from the app list now properly opens the Hub or app carousel instead of being blocked.
