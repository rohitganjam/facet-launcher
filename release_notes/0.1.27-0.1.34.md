# Facet Launcher 0.1.27 → 0.1.34

Combined release notes covering versions 0.1.27 through 0.1.34.

## New Features

**Landscape**
- Landscape mode for Home: clock, app list and dock sit side by side on phones, unfolded foldables and tablets. On short screens the dock sits down the right edge.
- Landscape layout for the Hub: widgets sit in a sideways-scrolling grid you can reorder, resize or remove. Its order is kept separate from portrait.
- Adjust mode works in landscape: drag the divider, choose top/middle/bottom for the clock, and resize it with corner handles.
- Landscape remembers its own clock position, alignment, size and app list alignment. Until you change them, it uses your portrait settings.
- New "Allow landscape mode" switch under Dock & Apps appearance (on by default).

**Weather**
- Weather icon and temperature next to the alarm and battery under the clock. Tap it for a 24-hour forecast, current details and the coming days' outlook (data from MET Norway).
- Clock accessories settings: turn alarm, battery and weather on or off and pick temperature units, for the whole launcher or per facet, with a live preview.
- Weather can use your current location or a city you search for. City search works offline from a built-in list of 34,000+ cities.
- Save up to 10 cities and switch between them with one tap.
- Optional coloured weather icons.
- Weather settings and saved cities are included in backups.

**Clock stack**
- Each facet's clock area can hold several pages (clock, widgets). Swipe sideways to move between them; page dots show during and just after a swipe.
- New Widget stack screen to reorder, configure, remove and add pages. Free users get one page, Pro up to five.
- Each facet remembers the page it last showed. A page whose app was uninstalled shows a placeholder with a Remove button.

**Notifications**
- Ongoing notifications (music, downloads) now show when you long-press an app. Tap to open; they can't be swiped away, and "Clear all" skips them.
- Notification settings have a live preview of the badge style and media player, and show an "Allow access" prompt when needed.

**Other**
- A small "What's new" pill appears once on Home after an update (not on fresh installs).
- App drawer settings have their own screen with a live drawer preview.
- App drawer grid size is now a Grid density setting (Compact, Regular, Spacious) that adapts to your screen.

## Fixes
- Fixed a crash opening an app picker on a phone with a Work Profile when the same app is in both profiles; each copy can now be added to Dock/Favorites separately.
- Fixed apps that showed a notification badge but an empty notification list.
- Fixed the setup screen flashing or reappearing at launch.
- Fixed backups corrupting when saved over an older, larger file; damaged backups can now be restored.
- Fixed the app picker search missing letters or freezing after changing sort order.
- Centre alignment: the notification badge no longer pushes the row off-centre.
- Fixed the facet carousel pushing settings and page dots off screen on tablets and foldables.
- Fixed custom clock widget jiggling in adjust mode, spilling off screen, and clock handle glitches.
- Previews now match your real Home, including clock position, clock style and the alarm/battery/weather row.
- Fixed landscape layout issues: cut-off facet buttons and "Add facet" card, clipped rows in the clock menu and set-default sheets, and the large gap between clock and calendar.
- Dragging back up in the contact connections sheet scrolls the list instead of closing the drawer.
- Folders in the drawer grid are now centred like app icons.
- Responsive widgets on Home now get their real size. Swiping between clock pages no longer flickers, jitters or reloads widgets, and horizontal drags on non-scrolling widgets pass through to Home.

## Improvements
- Work apps show the Work badge on Home, Dock, pickers, folders and settings.
- Notification badges sit on the icon corner for icon-only rows.
- Appearance settings reorganised into Theme, Icons and Fonts, with a font preview; several settings renamed for clarity ("Appearance", "Status bar icons", "Default", "Launcher Font Style").
- Settings screens and sheets no longer change with the in-app font size/weight options.
- New folder contents sheet that fills the screen with a matching header and slide animation.
- Widget picker sorted by app name, then widget name.
- Custom clock widgets can be resized down to one Hub grid cell.
- Backups now include clock, app list and landscape settings and landscape Hub order (older backups still restore).
- Clock alignment, size and divider are saved per facet and no longer change with launcher-wide settings; "Reset clock position" clears only placement.
- Clock menu shows options matching the visible page; replacing a page no longer requires Pro; new facets start with the native clock.
- Clock area defaults to about 40% of the screen; weather appears faster after unlock.
- Faster Home and startup; automations use less battery; album art uses less memory.
- On short screens, facet settings and delete move into a three-dot menu; the landscape clock side scrolls when content doesn't fit.
- New strings translated into all five languages.
