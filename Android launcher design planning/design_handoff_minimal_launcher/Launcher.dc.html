briefMinimal Android launcher — assumptions before you read the screens
SCOPE OF THIS PASS

Home (clock + calendar + favorites + dock) and App Drawer (list, grid, rail, search) only, per your pick. Turn 2 adds the long-press sheet and settings, turn 3 the profile carousel and per-profile settings, turn 4 the hub, first run, pickers and denied states.

Option 1a is a working prototype — drag up to open the drawer, drag down at the top to close, long-press empty space for the sheet (Switch profile, Edit profile), type to search, drag the rail to jump. The rest are static hi-fi to compare treatments.

DESIGN REASONING

You asked for soft, calm minimalism. So: one flat wallpaper tone, no cards or containers on the home surface, hairline dividers only in the drawer, and a single accent — taken from Material You, so it follows the wallpaper — used in exactly three places — active rail letter, notification dot, drawer selection. Type carries the hierarchy: a 200-weight clock against 400-weight app names.

Drawer is a translucent overlay on the wallpaper (F10) at 86% rather than blurred, so the home surface stays legible underneath without a second background asset.

PLACEHOLDERS & GAPS

App icons are monogram tiles, not real icons — this doubles as F11's monochrome-overlay mode. Drop real assets in and the "system default" mode replaces them. App names are invented; no real products are depicted.

Open from the PRD and not resolved here: the rail's behaviour at large system font scales, and the guided widget re-add when restoring a backup.
